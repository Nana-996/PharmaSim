Clinical Encounters

  1. Triage with Empathy: Patients often hide symptoms or daily supplements unless asked carefully.
  2. Check Polypharmacy: Watch for chelation, liver clearance, and narrow therapeutic index drugs.
  3. Prescriber Phone Calls: When a doctor makes a dosage error or an Rx looks forged, use the pharmacy phone to verify before dispensing.
  4. Consequences & Rent: Unethical sales earn quick cash but destroy your license. Keep an eye on your rent deadline!
